
# Held wiki draft: phone fob enrollment

Unreleased: hold until API/app deployment and hardware acceptance.

Open Register Fob/Replace Fob or Issue Fob. Check the contract and photo ID/address.
Choose Continue in Android app, sign in and confirm ID again. Scan and follow the
programming and second-tap verification prompts. Keep the same card for retries.

Without the app, choose Use enrollment reader instead. Scan the UID or use Import
New Key after a front-door scan. Submit, then present the card to an enrollment-enabled
reader. A replacement assigned this way disables the old fob immediately. Arrange
reader access first. UID assignment alone does not mean the fob is ready. Ask an
authorized operator if no enabled reader is available. Use test identities only.
